Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Auracard is made by Rakesh Rajbali. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Auracard has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no ads. Your quiz answers and aura readings are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

Aura Pass subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Photos

If you tap Save, Auracard asks permission to add your aura card image to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

Deleting your data

Open Auracard > Settings > Delete all readings, or delete the app. Both remove everything Auracard stored.

Children

Auracard does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
